# Attendance Policy

Minimum attendance requirements, condonation rules, medical leave and the consequences of falling
below the attendance bar. This revision took effect on 1 January 2025 and supersedes the earlier
75 percent bar.

## Minimum attendance

Every student must maintain at least 80 percent attendance in each course, computed from the first
day of the term. A course is counted only if the student is on its current registration list;
attendance is not retroactively credited for courses added late.

## Condonation

Attendance between 70 and 79 percent may be condoned by a maximum of 10 percent, and only for a
certified medical reason. Condonation is applied once per course per term and is granted only when
the medical certificate covers the exact dates of absence. A student below 70 percent after
condonation is not eligible for a second condonation in the same term.

## Detention

A student below 70 percent attendance in any course, after all condonations, is detained in that
course and cannot sit for its end-term examination. A detained student must repeat the course in a
later term paying Rs 700 as a course audit fee. Detention in three or more courses in one term
places the student on an academic probation review the following term.

## Hostel priority

A student detained in two or more courses in one term loses hostel room priority for the following
term, and is allotted a room only after every student with a clear record has been accommodated.

## Approved absence

Absence for institute duty such as representing the institute in sports, NCC or approved
competitions is not counted against attendance, provided duty letters are submitted to the faculty
within 3 working days of return. Absence without such a letter is unconditionally counted.

## Medical leave

Medical leave of up to 15 days per semester may be sanctioned with a certificate from the campus
dispensary or a registered hospital. Anything beyond 15 days leads to a semester drop, processed by
the academic section and recorded on the transcript as a leave of absence.
